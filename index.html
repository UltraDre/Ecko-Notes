      Clipboard is empty

      Press Grab to pull in whatever is on your system clipboard, or copy something inside Ecko.

      Browsers don’t let a web page watch your system clipboard, so this history fills from Grab and from copying or pasting inside Ecko — never silently in the background.

      Floating above everything

      Your notepad is pinned on top of every other window in an always-on-top Document Picture-in-Picture window. It stays live and keeps saving while you work elsewhere.

      Closing the floating window does the same thing.
